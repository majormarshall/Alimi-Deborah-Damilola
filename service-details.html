Professional Media Services

Every project is approached with creativity, precision and a commitment to delivering compelling visual content that meets and exceeds client expectations. Based in Ibadan, available across Nigeria.

Photography & Visual Storytelling Services

Alimi Deborah Damilola offers a comprehensive range of photography and media production services, tailored to individuals, businesses, organisations, and media houses looking to produce high-quality visual content.

  • Portrait & Event Photography — Weddings, birthdays, religious gatherings, corporate events and more.
  • Product Photography — Clean, commercial-grade product images for e-commerce, branding, and advertising.
  • Studio Photography — Controlled studio sessions for portraits, headshots and creative concepts.

Videography

Professional video shooting for events, social media content, brand promotions and documentary-style productions. Available for full-day and half-day coverage using professional cameras and smartphones depending on client needs.

Video Editing with CapCut

Post-production video editing using CapCut, delivering polished, engaging videos optimised for social media platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) and broadcast-style content. Includes colour grading, music sync, transitions and text overlays.

Photo Editing & Graphic Design

Image retouching, colour correction, background removal and creative graphic design using Adobe Photoshop. Ideal for profile images, event flyers, product images and branding materials.

Media Production Support

Drawing from hands-on experience at Africa Independent Television (AIT) and Voice FM 89.9, Deborah provides behind-the-scenes production support including studio preparation, props setup, programme logging, and production coordination.

Guest & Event Coordination

End-to-end event and session coordination — from scheduling and guest briefings to on-site management during photography or broadcast assignments. Ensures smooth, organised and professional delivery every time.
